St George's Day Countdown

Our St George's Day Countdown shows exactly how long is left until St George's Day. The live timer counts down the days, hours, minutes and seconds to 23 April.

St George's Day on 23 April is the feast day of Saint George, the patron saint of England, and it is also traditionally the date when William Shakespeare was born and the date on which he died in 1616. The English flag, the red cross of St George on a white background, is flown from churches and pubs, and roses, the national flower, are worn. It is not a bank holiday in England, although campaigns for one have been made.

Celebrating St George's Day

Many towns hold parades, fairs and pub events, and the day is a good excuse for a themed party or a British food night. The countdown gives you time to plan and to order flags and decorations.
