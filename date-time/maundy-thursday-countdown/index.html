Maundy Thursday Countdown

Our Maundy Thursday Countdown shows exactly how long is left until Maundy Thursday. The live timer counts down the days, hours, minutes and seconds to the Thursday before Easter Sunday.

Maundy Thursday is the Thursday before Easter and commemorates the Last Supper of Jesus and his disciples. "Maundy" comes from the Latin mandatum, "commandment", from the words "a new commandment I give unto you". In England the monarch traditionally hands out specially minted Maundy money at the Royal Maundy service, to as many men and as many women as the monarch's age in years.

Three days to Easter

Maundy Thursday falls three days before Easter Sunday, and for many people it is the last working day before the Easter break. It is a good moment to check that any Easter deliveries and bookings are in place.
