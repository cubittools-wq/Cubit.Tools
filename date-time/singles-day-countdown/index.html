Singles' Day Countdown

Our Singles' Day Countdown shows exactly how long is left until Singles' Day. The live timer counts down the days, hours, minutes and seconds to 11 November.

Singles' Day, also called Double 11, takes place on 11 November, chosen because the date, 11/11, is made up of four ones, representing single people. It started among university students in China in the 1990s and was turned into a shopping festival by Alibaba in 2009. Today it is one of the world's biggest online shopping events, and platforms such as Tmall, JD.com and AliExpress run large sales, with cross-border retailers taking part.
