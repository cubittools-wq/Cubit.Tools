Mother's Day Countdown (UK)

Our Mother's Day Countdown (UK) shows exactly how long is left until Mothering Sunday (UK Mother's Day). The live timer counts down the days, hours, minutes and seconds to the fourth Sunday of Lent, three weeks before Easter Sunday.

Mothering Sunday is the UK's Mother's Day, held on the fourth Sunday of Lent, exactly three weeks before Easter Sunday. It began as a religious tradition in which people returned to their "mother church", and later became a day for children to visit and thank their mothers, with flowers, cards and simnel cake. The UK and Ireland celebrate on this date, while the United States and many other countries celebrate Mother's Day in May.

Mothering Sunday is one of the busiest days of the year for florists, restaurants and card shops, and delivery slots fill quickly. Because the date moves with Easter, between 1 March and 4 April, the countdown is a useful reminder that it can be earlier than you think.
