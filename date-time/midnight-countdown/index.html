Countdown to Midnight

Our Countdown to Midnight shows exactly how long is left until midnight. The live timer counts down the days, hours, minutes and seconds to 12am each day, in your own time zone.

The midnight countdown shows how long remains until the end of the day, at 12 o'clock at night, when the date changes. It resets automatically every day, so you can leave the page open and it will start again at midnight.

Midnight is the start of a new calendar day in most of the world, though the exact moment depends on your time zone. It is used for deadlines, competition cut-offs, sales that end "at midnight" and the moment when many things renew or expire.

How Long Until Midnight?

Whichever day you visit, the countdown above shows how much time remains. As soon as the day has passed, it moves on to the next one automatically. The countdown ends at 12am each day, in your own time zone.

Deadlines that end at midnight

Entries, offers and sales often close at midnight, but check the terms: sometimes this means the start of the day, 00:00, rather than the end, 23:59. The countdown uses your device's time zone.
