Early May Bank Holiday Countdown

Our Early May Bank Holiday Countdown shows exactly how long is left until the Early May bank holiday. The live timer counts down the days, hours, minutes and seconds to the first Monday in May.

The Early May bank holiday falls on the first Monday in May and is observed in England, Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland. It was introduced in 1978 and is often linked to May Day, the ancient spring festival with maypoles and Morris dancing, and to International Workers' Day on 1 May in many other countries. On rare occasions it has been moved, as in 2020, when it took place on Friday 8 May to mark the 75th anniversary of VE Day.

The first long weekend of spring

For many people the Early May weekend is one of the first chances of the year for a short break, a garden centre trip or a bank holiday barbecue, so accommodation and travel can be more expensive. The countdown is a helpful reminder to book early.
