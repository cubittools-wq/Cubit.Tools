Birthday Countdown

How long until your next birthday?

Enter your date of birth above and the countdown works out your next birthday automatically, showing the days, hours, minutes and seconds remaining, and the age you will be turning. If your birthday is today, it shows a birthday message.

Leap day birthdays

People born on 29 February have a real birthday only once every four years. In other years, this countdown counts down to 1 March, which is the legal birthday for leaplings in the UK. If your birthday is on 29 February, you can also count down to the next leap day with our Leap Day Countdown.

Counting for someone else

You can use the counter for a friend, a child or a partner by entering their date of birth instead of your own. Whatever you enter stays in your own browser, so the countdown is still there the next time you visit this page on the same device, and nothing is sent to us.
