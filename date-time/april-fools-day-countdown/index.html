April Fools' Day Countdown

Our April Fools' Day Countdown shows exactly how long is left until April Fools' Day. The live timer counts down the days, hours, minutes and seconds to 1 April.

April Fools' Day, on 1 April, is a day of practical jokes and hoaxes around the world. The origins are uncertain, but the tradition has been recorded in Europe for hundreds of years. In the UK the custom is that pranks must end at noon, and anyone who plays one after that is the "fool" themselves. One of the most famous media hoaxes was the BBC's 1957 Panorama report about Swiss farmers harvesting spaghetti from trees.
