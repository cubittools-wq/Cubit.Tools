Home Time Countdown

Counting down to the end of the working day

Set the time you finish work and the countdown shows the hours, minutes and seconds left until home time. It resets to the same time tomorrow once the day is done, so you can keep the page open and use it every day.

Home time for school, too

The countdown works for any daily time, such as the end of a school day, the end of a shift, the time you pick up the children, or the time the shops shut. Change the time whenever you need to.

Make the last hour count

The last hour of the day is a good time to write tomorrow's to-do list, clear your inbox and tidy your desk, so that you leave on time and start the next day with a clear head. Whatever you enter stays in your own browser, so the countdown is still there the next time you visit this page on the same device, and nothing is sent to us.

How the countdown works

The counter checks the current time on your device against your finishing time. Before that time it counts down to today's finish; after it, it counts down to the same time tomorrow. With the default finish of 17:00, at 14:22 and 30 seconds the counter shows 2 hours, 37 minutes and 30 seconds.

Questions and answers

I work nights. Can I count down to 6:00 am?

Yes. Enter 06:00 and the counter runs through midnight to your next 6:00 am finish. The same applies to any shift that ends after midnight.

Does it skip weekends?

No. It counts down to your finishing time every day, so on days off you can close the page, or set a different time.

Can I use it for school pick-up?

Yes. Enter the pick-up time instead of a finishing time, and the countdown resets each day in the same way.
