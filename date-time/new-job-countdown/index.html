New Job Countdown

Counting down to your first day

Whether you are leaving a job you dislike or looking forward to a fresh start, the countdown shows the days, hours, minutes and seconds until your first day. It is also useful for counting the days left in your notice period, by entering your last day instead.

Before you start

Use the weeks before your new job to take a proper break, complete any paperwork the employer has requested, check the commute, and sort out your finances, such as the date of your first pay. If you can, read up on the company, the team and the products so that you can hit the ground running.

Working out your notice

Most UK employees have to give at least the notice set out in their contract, and the statutory minimum is one week for those who have worked for a month or more. Check your contract, and use the counter to keep track. Whatever you enter stays in your own browser, so the countdown is still there the next time you visit this page on the same device, and nothing is sent to us.
