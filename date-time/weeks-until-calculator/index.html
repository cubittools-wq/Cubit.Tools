Weeks Until Calculator

How many weeks until a date?

Choose a date above and the line under the timer shows the time as weeks and days, for example 12 weeks and 3 days, alongside the live countdown in days, hours, minutes and seconds. For very long waits it switches to years, months and days.

Why count in weeks?

Weeks are a natural unit for plans that build up over time: training for a race, a diet or exercise programme, a pregnancy, a savings goal, a revision timetable or a project with weekly milestones. Counting in weeks makes it easy to split the time into equal blocks.

Keep it handy

Whatever you enter stays in your own browser, so the countdown is still there the next time you visit this page on the same device, and nothing is sent to us.

How the weeks are worked out

The calculator finds the time left until the start of the chosen date, takes the whole days, and divides them by 7. The whole number is the weeks, and the remainder is the extra days. For example, from the start of 1 May, 30 July is 90 days away. 90 Ã· 7 = 12 remainder 6, so the summary shows 12 weeks and 6 days.

Questions and answers

Does it count full weeks or round up?

It shows full weeks only, with the remaining days listed separately. A wait of 6 weeks and 6 days is shown that way, not as 7 weeks.

What if the date is less than a week away?

The weeks line only appears for 7 days or more. For shorter waits, read the days, hours and minutes on the timer.

How can I use it for a training plan?

Check that the weeks shown are enough for your plan. A typical beginner's 5K plan takes 9 weeks, so if the counter shows fewer than 9 weeks before your race, choose a shorter plan or a later race.
