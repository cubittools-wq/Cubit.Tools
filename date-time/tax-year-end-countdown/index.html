Tax Year End Countdown

Our Tax Year End Countdown shows exactly how long is left until the end of the tax year. The live timer counts down the days, hours, minutes and seconds to 11:59pm on 5 April.

The UK tax year runs from 6 April to 5 April the following year, an unusual date that dates back to the switch to the Gregorian calendar in 1752. Many tax allowances and reliefs work on a use-it-or-lose-it basis and cannot be carried over, including ISA subscriptions and the capital gains tax annual exempt amount, so the end of the tax year is an important planning date for individuals and businesses.

How Many Days Until the End of the Tax Year?

Whichever day you visit, the countdown above shows how much time remains. As soon as the day has passed, it moves on to the next one automatically. The countdown ends at 11:59pm on 5 April.

Get your finances in order

Before 5 April you may want to review your ISA and pension contributions, capital gains and any charitable giving. This is general information and not financial or tax advice; check GOV.UK or speak to a qualified adviser about your own position.
