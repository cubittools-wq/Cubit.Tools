Retirement Countdown

How long until you retire?

Enter your date of birth and the age at which you plan to retire, and the counter works out the date of your retirement birthday and counts down to it. It shows the days, hours, minutes and seconds and also the time in years, months and days, which is the easiest way to see a countdown that may be decades long.

State pension ages

The age you retire is your choice, but state pensions start at a set age. In the UK the State Pension age is rising from 66 to 67 between 2026 and 2028, and in the United States the full retirement age for Social Security is 67 for anyone born in 1960 or later. Check the official government website for your own pension age, as it depends on your date of birth.

Planning for the day

A retirement countdown is a good prompt to review your pension, savings and plans for how you will spend your time. This tool is for information only and is not financial advice. Whatever you enter stays in your own browser, so the countdown is still there the next time you visit this page on the same device, and nothing is sent to us.
