Star Wars Day Countdown

Our Star Wars Day Countdown shows exactly how long is left until Star Wars Day. The live timer counts down the days, hours, minutes and seconds to 4 May.

Star Wars Day is celebrated on 4 May by fans of the Star Wars films, because the date sounds like the famous phrase "May the Force be with you". An early use of the pun appeared in 1979, when the UK Conservative Party placed a newspaper advert reading "May the Fourth Be With You, Maggie" to congratulate Margaret Thatcher on her election victory. Today the day is marked with marathons, cosplay, special events and promotions.
