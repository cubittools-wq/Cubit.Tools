Chinese New Year Countdown

Our Chinese New Year Countdown shows exactly how long is left until Chinese New Year. The live timer counts down the days, hours, minutes and seconds to a date between 21 January and 20 February set by the lunisolar calendar.

Chinese New Year, also called the Lunar New Year or Spring Festival, is the most important holiday in China and is celebrated across East and Southeast Asia and by communities around the world. It follows the lunisolar calendar, so the date changes each year, always between 21 January and 20 February. Each year is linked to one of twelve zodiac animals, and celebrations traditionally last fifteen days, ending with the Lantern Festival.

Families gather for a reunion dinner on New Year's Eve, homes are cleaned and decorated in red, and children receive red envelopes containing money. Dragon and lion dances, firecrackers and fireworks are a highlight of the festivities, and London's Chinatown hosts one of the biggest celebrations outside Asia.
