Columbus Day Countdown

Our Columbus Day Countdown shows exactly how long is left until Columbus Day (Indigenous Peoples' Day). The live timer counts down the days, hours, minutes and seconds to the second Monday in October.

Columbus Day is a United States federal holiday on the second Monday in October, marking the arrival of Christopher Columbus in the Americas in 1492. It has been a federal holiday since 1937 and a Monday holiday since 1971. Many states and cities now observe Indigenous Peoples' Day on the same date, either instead of or alongside Columbus Day, to honour Native American history and culture.

The same day in Canada

The same Monday is Thanksgiving in Canada, so it is a long weekend on both sides of the border. Schools, banks and government offices in the US may be closed, but the post office and many stores stay open.
