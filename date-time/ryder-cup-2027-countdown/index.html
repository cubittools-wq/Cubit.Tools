Ryder Cup 2027 Countdown

Our Ryder Cup 2027 Countdown shows exactly how long is left until the 2027 Ryder Cup. The live timer counts down the days, hours, minutes and seconds to 17 September 2027, the first day.

The 2027 Ryder Cup, the 46th edition of the biennial golf contest between Europe and the United States, will be played at Adare Manor in County Limerick, Ireland, from 17 to 19 September 2027. It will be the first Ryder Cup in Ireland since 2006, when the K Club in County Kildare hosted.

How Many Days Until the Ryder Cup 2027?

Whichever day you visit, the countdown above shows how much time remains. The countdown above ends on 17 September 2027, the first day.

Planning to be there

Tickets and hotel rooms around Limerick are in high demand for the Ryder Cup, so the months beforehand are a helpful planning window. The countdown is to the first day of play.
