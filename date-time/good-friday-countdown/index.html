Good Friday Countdown

Our Good Friday Countdown shows exactly how long is left until Good Friday. The live timer counts down the days, hours, minutes and seconds to the Friday before Easter Sunday.

Good Friday is the Friday before Easter Sunday, when Christians remember the crucifixion of Jesus. It is a bank holiday in the UK and a public holiday in many other countries, including Australia, Canada and Germany. Traditional foods include hot cross buns and fish, and many churches hold quiet services or processions.

How Many Days Until Good Friday?

A long weekend to plan for

Because Good Friday starts a four-day weekend, it is one of the busiest travel days of the spring. It is also a day when some shops, banks and public services close, so it is worth knowing how far away it is before you make plans.
