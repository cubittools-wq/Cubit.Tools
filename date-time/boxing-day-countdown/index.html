Boxing Day Countdown

Our Boxing Day Countdown shows exactly how long is left until Boxing Day. The live timer counts down the days, hours, minutes and seconds to 26 December.

Boxing Day is 26 December, the day after Christmas Day. It is a public holiday in the UK, Canada, Australia, New Zealand and several other Commonwealth countries. The name is usually linked to the old custom of giving boxes of gifts or money to servants and tradespeople, or to the alms boxes that churches opened for the poor. Today it is best known for the start of the winter sales and a full programme of sport, including Premier League football and the King George VI Chase at Kempton Park.

How Many Days Until Boxing Day?

When the bank holiday moves

If 26 December falls on a weekend, the Boxing Day bank holiday is moved to the next working day, but this countdown always points to 26 December itself. If you are looking for the day off work, use our Next Bank Holiday Countdown instead.
