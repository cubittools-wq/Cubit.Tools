Time Until Calculator

Time until a date and time

The time until calculator answers the question of exactly how long there is until something happens. Enter the date and time above and the timer breaks the answer into days, hours, minutes and seconds, then adds the equivalent in weeks or in years, months and days.

Working out the difference by hand

To do it by hand you need to work out the days between the dates, allowing for months of different lengths, and then the hours and minutes on top. The calculator does this for you, including leap years and changes of clocks.

Related tools

To find the number of days between two dates in the past or future, use our days between dates calculator. To find your age, use our age calculator. Whatever you enter stays in your own browser, so the countdown is still there the next time you visit this page on the same device, and nothing is sent to us.

Worked example

At 8:30 am on 2 February, the time until 12:00 noon on 9 February is 7 days, 3 hours and 30 minutes. By hand, you count the whole days first (2 to 9 February is 7 days), then the time of day on top (8:30 am to 12:00 noon is 3 hours and 30 minutes).

Questions and answers

What is the difference between this and the days between dates calculator?

This calculator counts down live from now to one future moment, including the time of day. The days between dates calculator compares any two dates, past or future, in whole days.

Can I enter a time in the past?

No. The countdown needs a future date and time. For the time since a past date, use the days between dates calculator.
