Countdown to 2030

Our Countdown to 2030 shows exactly how long is left until 2030. The live timer counts down the days, hours, minutes and seconds to 1 January 2030.

2030 is a milestone year. It is the target date of the United Nations 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development, with its 17 Sustainable Development Goals, and a date that many governments and companies have chosen for their climate and net-zero targets. It is also the start of a new decade. The countdown ends at midnight at the start of 1 January 2030.

How Many Days Until 2030?
