Clocks Go Forward Countdown (UK)

Our Clocks Go Forward Countdown (UK) shows exactly how long is left until the clocks going forward. The live timer counts down the days, hours, minutes and seconds to 1am on the last Sunday in March.

In the UK the clocks go forward by one hour at 1am on the last Sunday in March, when Greenwich Mean Time changes to British Summer Time. The clocks change at 1am, so the night is an hour shorter, but the evenings are lighter for the rest of the spring and summer. The saying "spring forward, fall back" helps to remember which way they go.

British Summer Time was introduced in 1916, following a campaign by the builder William Willett, who argued that people were wasting daylight in summer mornings.

When Do the Clocks Go Forward?

Don't lose an hour

The clocks change at 1am GMT, which is 1am UTC, and the countdown shows the time in your own time zone. The same dates apply across the European Union. The target time shown on the countdown is the new clock time straight after the change, so in the UK it reads 2am BST. Many phones and computers update automatically, but wall clocks, ovens, cars and watches need changing by hand.
