Notting Hill Carnival Countdown

Our Notting Hill Carnival Countdown shows exactly how long is left until Notting Hill Carnival. The live timer counts down the days, hours, minutes and seconds to the Sunday of the August bank holiday weekend.

Notting Hill Carnival is one of the biggest street festivals in the world, held every year in west London over the August bank holiday weekend. It grew out of the Caribbean community's celebrations in the 1960s and features costumed parades, steel bands, sound systems and Caribbean food, with hundreds of thousands of visitors over the weekend. The Sunday is traditionally Family Day and the Monday is the main parade day.
