Weekend Countdown

Our Weekend Countdown shows exactly how long is left until the weekend. The live timer counts down the days, hours, minutes and seconds to 5pm on Friday.

The weekend countdown ticks down to 5pm on Friday, the moment when many people in the UK and elsewhere finish work and the weekend begins. Whether you are counting the hours to the end of a busy week or planning something for Friday night, the timer shows exactly how long you have left.

The five-day working week, with Saturday and Sunday as the weekend, became widespread in the twentieth century, following the introduction of the Saturday half-holiday and later the full two-day weekend in factories and offices. In some countries the weekend falls on different days, such as Friday and Saturday in parts of the Middle East.

How Long Until the Weekend?

Whichever day you visit, the countdown above shows how much time remains. As soon as the day has passed, it moves on to the next one automatically. The countdown ends at 5pm on Friday.

Beat the Friday afternoon

The countdown resets automatically each week. Use it to pace yourself through the week, to plan an early finish, or to time the moment for a well-earned drink. If it is already the weekend, it counts down to the following Friday at 5pm.
