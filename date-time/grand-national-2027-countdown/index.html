Grand National 2027 Countdown

Our Grand National 2027 Countdown shows exactly how long is left until the 2027 Grand National. The live timer counts down the days, hours, minutes and seconds to Saturday 10 April 2027.

The Grand National is the most famous horse race in Britain, run at Aintree Racecourse near Liverpool. The 2027 Grand National Festival runs from 8 to 10 April, and the big race, over about four and a quarter miles with 30 fences to jump, takes place on Grand National Day, Saturday 10 April 2027. The race was first run in 1839, and since 2024 the maximum field has been 34 runners.

How Many Days Until the Grand National?

Whichever day you visit, the countdown above shows how much time remains. The countdown above ends on Saturday 10 April 2027.

Key date

  • Saturday 10 April 2027

Day by day

The festival opens on Thursday 8 April, with Ladies Day on the Friday and the Grand National itself on the Saturday. The countdown is to the start of Grand National Day.
