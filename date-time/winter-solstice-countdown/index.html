Winter Solstice Countdown

Our Winter Solstice Countdown shows exactly how long is left until the December solstice. The live timer counts down the days, hours, minutes and seconds to the moment of the December solstice.

The December solstice is the moment when the sun reaches its southernmost point in the sky, giving the shortest day of the year in the northern hemisphere and the longest in the southern hemisphere. It marks the astronomical start of winter. In the UK, daylight lasts only around eight hours in the south, and less than that in Scotland, but the days start to lengthen again from this point.

Midwinter has been celebrated for thousands of years, from the alignments at Stonehenge and Newgrange to the Roman festival of Saturnalia, the Norse Yule, and the Dongzhi festival in East Asia.

How Many Days Until the Winter Solstice?

Whichever day you visit, the countdown above shows how much time remains. The countdown ends at the moment of the December solstice.

The shortest day

The exact time of the solstice is calculated in UTC and shown to the nearest minute. Although the solstice is the shortest day, the earliest sunsets come a couple of weeks before it in the northern hemisphere and the latest sunrises a couple of weeks after, so the coldest weather still lies ahead. Dates and seasons given are for the northern hemisphere. In the southern hemisphere the seasons are reversed, so the same date marks the start of the opposite season.
