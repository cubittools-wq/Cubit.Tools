Mardi Gras Countdown

Our Mardi Gras Countdown shows exactly how long is left until Mardi Gras. The live timer counts down the days, hours, minutes and seconds to the Tuesday 47 days before Easter Sunday.

Mardi Gras is French for "Fat Tuesday", the last day of the Carnival season before the start of Lent on Ash Wednesday. It is celebrated with parades, masks, music and feasting in places such as New Orleans, Mobile in Alabama, Rio de Janeiro and Venice. In New Orleans the official colours are purple, green and gold, standing for justice, faith and power, and the season is marked by king cakes.

A moving date

Mardi Gras falls on the same day as Shrove Tuesday, always 47 days before Easter Sunday, so it can be as early as 3 February or as late as 9 March. The Carnival season itself begins on 6 January, so the biggest parades often happen in the days and weeks before the day itself.
