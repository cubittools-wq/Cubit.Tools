Autumn Equinox Countdown

Our Autumn Equinox Countdown shows exactly how long is left until the September equinox. The live timer counts down the days, hours, minutes and seconds to the moment of the September equinox.

The September equinox is the moment when the sun crosses the celestial equator heading south, marking the astronomical start of autumn in the northern hemisphere and of spring in the southern hemisphere. Day and night are close to equal in length on the date of the equinox. It usually falls on 22 or 23 September and is the moment counted down to here, shown to the nearest minute.

The September equinox is closely linked to harvest festivals, and the full moon nearest to it is known as the Harvest Moon.

How Many Days Until the Autumn Equinox?

Whichever day you visit, the countdown above shows how much time remains. The countdown ends at the moment of the September equinox.

Equinox and the seasons

The times are calculated in UTC and shown in your local time on the countdown. The meteorological start of autumn is 1 September, which is a different date from the astronomical start on the equinox. Dates and seasons given are for the northern hemisphere. In the southern hemisphere the seasons are reversed, so the same date marks the start of the opposite season.
