Pi Day Countdown

Our Pi Day Countdown shows exactly how long is left until Pi Day. The live timer counts down the days, hours, minutes and seconds to 14 March.

Pi Day is celebrated on 14 March, because the date written month-first, 3/14, matches the first three digits of pi, 3.14. It was started in 1988 by the physicist Larry Shaw at the San Francisco Exploratorium, where staff and visitors marched around a circle and ate fruit pie. In 2009 the US House of Representatives recognised the day. 14 March is also the birthday of Albert Einstein, who was born in 1879.

Pie and pi

Traditions include eating pie, reciting digits of pi and running maths events in schools. In the UK, where dates are written day-first, some celebrate Pi Approximation Day on 22 July, since 22/7 is a well-known fraction close to pi.
