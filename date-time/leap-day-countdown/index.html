Leap Day Countdown

Our Leap Day Countdown shows exactly how long is left until the next Leap Day. The live timer counts down the days, hours, minutes and seconds to 29 February in leap years.

A Leap Day is the extra day, 29 February, added to the calendar in most years divisible by four to keep it in step with the Earth's orbit around the sun. A year takes about 365.2422 days, so without a leap day the calendar would drift by about a day every four years. Years divisible by 100 are not leap years unless they are also divisible by 400, so 2000 was a leap year but 1900 and 2100 are not.

People born on 29 February are known as leaplings or leapers, and often celebrate their birthday on 28 February or 1 March in other years. In some traditions, leap day is the one day when women may propose marriage.
