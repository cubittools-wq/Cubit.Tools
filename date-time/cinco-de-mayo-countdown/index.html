Cinco de Mayo Countdown

Our Cinco de Mayo Countdown shows exactly how long is left until Cinco de Mayo. The live timer counts down the days, hours, minutes and seconds to 5 May.

Cinco de Mayo, meaning "fifth of May", commemorates the victory of the Mexican army over French forces at the Battle of Puebla on 5 May 1862. Despite a common misunderstanding, it is not Mexico's Independence Day, which is on 16 September. It is celebrated most in the city of Puebla and, on a much larger scale, in the United States, where it has become a celebration of Mexican-American culture with parades, music, dancing and food.

Not Mexican Independence Day

Mexican Independence Day, on 16 September, is the country's main national holiday. Cinco de Mayo is a regional holiday in Puebla and a cultural celebration elsewhere. The countdown shows the time to the fifth of May.
