Total Solar Eclipse 2027 Countdown

Our Total Solar Eclipse 2027 Countdown shows exactly how long is left until the 2 August 2027 total solar eclipse. The live timer counts down the days, hours, minutes and seconds to 10:07 UTC on 2 August 2027, the moment of greatest eclipse.

On 2 August 2027 a total solar eclipse will cross southern Spain, Gibraltar, northern Africa, Egypt and Saudi Arabia, and the Arabian peninsula. It has been called the Eclipse of the Century, because the maximum duration of totality, 6 minutes and 23 seconds near Luxor in Egypt, is one of the longest of the century. The moment of greatest eclipse is at about 10:07 UTC.

Outside the path of totality, a partial eclipse will be visible across much of Europe, Africa and Asia. Never look at the sun without proper eclipse glasses.

Where to see it

The path of totality runs from Cadiz, Malaga and Almeria in Spain to Tangier, Oran and Benghazi in North Africa and on to Luxor, Jeddah and Mecca. Travel and accommodation along the path are likely to book up well in advance.
