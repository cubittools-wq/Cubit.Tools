India's Independence Day Countdown

Our India's Independence Day Countdown shows exactly how long is left until India's Independence Day. The live timer counts down the days, hours, minutes and seconds to 15 August.

Independence Day is celebrated in India on 15 August and marks the end of British rule in 1947. The Prime Minister raises the national flag at the Red Fort in Delhi and gives a televised address to the nation, and flag-hoisting ceremonies, parades and cultural events take place across the country. Kites are flown in many cities, and people wear the colours of the flag: saffron, white and green.
