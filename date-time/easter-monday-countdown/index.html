Easter Monday Countdown

Our Easter Monday Countdown shows exactly how long is left until Easter Monday. The live timer counts down the days, hours, minutes and seconds to the day after Easter Sunday.

Easter Monday is the day after Easter Sunday and a bank holiday in England, Wales and Northern Ireland, as well as a public holiday in countries such as Australia, France and Germany. Traditional events include egg rolling, such as the one held in Avenham Park in Preston, and in the United States the White House Easter Egg Roll takes place on the Monday after Easter.

How Many Days Until Easter Monday?

Whichever day you visit, the countdown above shows how much time remains. As soon as the day has passed, it moves on to the next one automatically. Easter Monday falls on the day after Easter Sunday.

End of the Easter break

For many people Easter Monday is the last day of the long weekend, so it is often the busiest day for travel home. If you have a day trip planned, allow extra time for traffic and public transport.
