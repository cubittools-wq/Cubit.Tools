Presidents' Day Countdown

Our Presidents' Day Countdown shows exactly how long is left until Presidents' Day. The live timer counts down the days, hours, minutes and seconds to the third Monday in February.

Presidents' Day is the popular name for Washington's Birthday, a United States federal holiday on the third Monday in February. It was originally held on 22 February, George Washington's birthday, and was moved to a Monday by the Uniform Monday Holiday Act, which took effect in 1971. Many people treat it as a day honouring all US presidents, and it is known for sales at car dealers, furniture stores and retailers.
