Canada Day Countdown

Our Canada Day Countdown shows exactly how long is left until Canada Day. The live timer counts down the days, hours, minutes and seconds to 1 July.

Canada Day is Canada's national day on 1 July, marking the anniversary of Confederation in 1867, when the British North America Act united the Province of Canada, Nova Scotia and New Brunswick into a single dominion. It was called Dominion Day until 1982. People celebrate with fireworks, parades, concerts and barbecues, wearing red and white. The biggest celebrations are held in Ottawa, on Parliament Hill.
