Christmas Eve Countdown

Our Christmas Eve Countdown shows exactly how long is left until Christmas Eve. The live timer counts down the days, hours, minutes and seconds to 24 December.

Christmas Eve is the evening and day before Christmas Day, on 24 December. In Germany, Poland and the Nordic countries it is the main night of the celebration, when families share a meal and open their presents. In the UK and Ireland it is more traditionally the night for hanging stockings, leaving a mince pie and a drink out for Father Christmas, and, for churchgoers, attending a carol service or Midnight Mass.

Christmas Eve is the busiest day of the year for last-minute shopping, food collections and wrapping. Use the timer to set yourself a deadline for the jobs that cannot slip, such as collecting the turkey or finishing a delivery run, and remember that many UK shops close early on 24 December.
