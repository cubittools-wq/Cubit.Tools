Sleeps Until Christmas Countdown

Our Sleeps Until Christmas Countdown shows exactly how long is left until Christmas Day. The live timer counts down the days, hours, minutes and seconds to 25 December.

Children rarely think in days, weeks or months, but "how many sleeps" is a measure they understand: every night's sleep brings Christmas morning one step closer. The timer above shows the number of sleeps left as well as the days, hours, minutes and seconds, so a child can check it every morning.

How Many Sleeps Until Christmas?

Whichever day you visit, the countdown above shows how much time remains. As soon as the day has passed, it moves on to the next one automatically. Christmas Day falls on 25 December.

Make a sleeps chain

A paper chain with one link for every sleep is a classic way to make the wait visible. Check the number of sleeps shown above, make that many links, and take one off each morning. Another idea is to write the number of sleeps on a whiteboard on the fridge and update it at breakfast.
