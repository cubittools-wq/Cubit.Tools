Rosh Hashanah Countdown

Our Rosh Hashanah Countdown shows exactly how long is left until Rosh Hashanah. The live timer counts down the days, hours, minutes and seconds to the first day of Tishrei, beginning at sunset the evening before.

Rosh Hashanah, the Jewish New Year, marks the start of the High Holy Days and the beginning of the Hebrew year. It falls on the first two days of the month of Tishrei and opens ten days of reflection and repentance that end with Yom Kippur. The shofar, a ram's horn, is sounded in the synagogue, and families share a festive meal that traditionally includes apples dipped in honey for a sweet year. The Hebrew year 5787 begins in September 2026 and 5788 in 2027.

The eve begins the festival

Because Jewish days run from sunset to sunset, Rosh Hashanah begins at nightfall on the evening before the first day. The countdown above is to that day, so use it as a reminder to prepare the meal, book synagogue tickets and send greetings.
