St David's Day Countdown

Our St David's Day Countdown shows exactly how long is left until St David's Day. The live timer counts down the days, hours, minutes and seconds to 1 March.

St David's Day, on 1 March, is the national day of Wales, in honour of Saint David, the patron saint of Wales, who died on that date around the year 589. People wear daffodils or leeks, both national emblems of Wales, and children in traditional Welsh costume take part in school events and parades. The Welsh greeting is "Dydd GÅµyl Dewi Hapus!". Cardiff holds a large parade, and Welsh cakes are a traditional treat. It is not a bank holiday.

Wear a daffodil or a leek

Daffodils are in flower in early March, which is why the flower became linked with the day, and the leek is the older emblem. The countdown is a useful reminder to arrange costumes, bake Welsh cakes or book a table.
