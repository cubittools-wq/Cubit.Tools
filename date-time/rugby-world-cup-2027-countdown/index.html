Rugby World Cup 2027 Countdown

Our Rugby World Cup 2027 Countdown shows exactly how long is left until the 2027 Rugby World Cup. The live timer counts down the days, hours, minutes and seconds to 1 October 2027, the opening match in Perth.

The 2027 Rugby World Cup will be held in Australia, from 1 October to 13 November 2027, with 24 teams competing, up from 20 in previous tournaments. The opening match is in Perth, and the final takes place at Stadium Australia in Sydney, which also hosts the semi-finals. It is the first men's Rugby World Cup in Australia since 2003.

How Many Days Until the Rugby World Cup 2027?

Whichever day you visit, the countdown above shows how much time remains. The countdown above ends on 1 October 2027, the opening match in Perth.

Time zones and planning

Australia is many hours ahead of the UK and Europe, so many matches will be played overnight or in the early morning there. The countdown is to the first day of the tournament; check World Rugby for the fixture list.
