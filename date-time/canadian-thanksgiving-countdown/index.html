Canadian Thanksgiving Countdown

Our Canadian Thanksgiving Countdown shows exactly how long is left until Canadian Thanksgiving. The live timer counts down the days, hours, minutes and seconds to the second Monday in October.

Canadian Thanksgiving is held on the second Monday in October, more than a month before the American one. The date was fixed in 1957 by Parliament. Canadians often trace the tradition to 1578, when the explorer Martin Frobisher held a ceremony of thanks in what is now Nunavut, and it is marked by a harvest meal with turkey, pumpkin pie and seasonal vegetables. Many families celebrate over the whole long weekend.
