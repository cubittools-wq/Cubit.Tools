Passover Countdown

Our Passover Countdown shows exactly how long is left until Passover. The live timer counts down the days, hours, minutes and seconds to the 15th of Nisan, beginning at sunset the evening before.

Passover, or Pesach, commemorates the liberation of the Israelites from slavery in ancient Egypt. It begins on the 15th day of the Hebrew month of Nisan, in spring, and lasts seven days in Israel and eight in most Jewish communities elsewhere. The festival opens with the Seder, a ritual meal in which the story of the Exodus is retold, and throughout it Jews avoid leavened bread and eat matzah, an unleavened flatbread.
