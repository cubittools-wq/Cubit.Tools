Date Countdown Calculator

Day countdown for any date

Choose a date and this countdown shows how many days are left until it, along with the hours, minutes and seconds. It works as a day countdown, a calendar countdown or a date countdown, whichever you call it, and it keeps going as you leave the page open.

Big round numbers

Counting down to a date is a good way to build up to a milestone. The line below the timer shows the wait in weeks and days, or in years, months and days, so you can see when you reach 100 days to go, 50 days to go or the last week.

Making the countdown work for you

Try setting a date that helps you rather than one to dread, such as the day a new habit starts, the start of a new term or the date a bill is paid off. Whatever you enter stays in your own browser, so the countdown is still there the next time you visit this page on the same device, and nothing is sent to us.
