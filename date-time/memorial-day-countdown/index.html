Memorial Day Countdown

Our Memorial Day Countdown shows exactly how long is left until Memorial Day. The live timer counts down the days, hours, minutes and seconds to the last Monday in May.

Memorial Day is a United States federal holiday on the last Monday in May, honouring the men and women who died while serving in the US military. It began after the Civil War as Decoration Day, when graves were decorated with flowers, and became an official Monday holiday in 1971. Many Americans visit cemeteries and memorials, and fly the flag at half-staff until noon. It is also the unofficial start of summer, with a long weekend of barbecues and travel.
