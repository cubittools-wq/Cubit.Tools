Winter Countdown

Our Winter Countdown shows exactly how long is left until Winter. The live timer counts down the days, hours, minutes and seconds to 1 December, the start of meteorological winter.

Meteorological winter begins on 1 December and runs until the end of February, covering the coldest three months of the year in the northern hemisphere. The astronomical start of winter is the December solstice, around 21 December, so the two dates differ by about three weeks. In December the days are at their shortest, and many people welcome the season with Christmas markets, advent calendars and winter walks. Dates and seasons given are for the northern hemisphere. In the southern hemisphere the seasons are reversed, so the same date marks the start of the opposite season.
