Oktoberfest is the world's largest beer festival, held each year on the Theresienwiese in Munich, Germany. It began in 1810 as a celebration of the wedding of Crown Prince Ludwig, later King Ludwig I of Bavaria, and Princess Therese. The festival opens at noon on the first Saturday when the mayor of Munich taps the first keg with the cry "O'zapft is!", meaning "it's tapped!". Dates of the festival

Despite the name, most of Oktoberfest takes place in September. It starts on the first Saturday on or after 16 September and runs for around sixteen to eighteen days, ending on the first Sunday in October, or on 3 October, German Unity Day, if that is later.
