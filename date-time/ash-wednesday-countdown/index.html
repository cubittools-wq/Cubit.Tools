Ash Wednesday Countdown

Our Ash Wednesday Countdown shows exactly how long is left until Ash Wednesday. The live timer counts down the days, hours, minutes and seconds to the Wednesday 46 days before Easter Sunday.

Ash Wednesday is the first day of Lent, the forty-day season of fasting and reflection before Easter, not counting Sundays. Many Christians attend a service at which ashes are marked on the forehead in the shape of a cross. It always falls 46 days before Easter Sunday, between 4 February and 10 March.
