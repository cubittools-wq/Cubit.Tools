Mother's Day Countdown (US)

Our Mother's Day Countdown (US) shows exactly how long is left until Mother's Day (US). The live timer counts down the days, hours, minutes and seconds to the second Sunday in May.

In the United States, Mother's Day is on the second Sunday in May. Anna Jarvis campaigned for a national day to honour mothers after the death of her own mother, and the first observance she organised was held in 1908. Woodrow Wilson made it an official national holiday in 1914. The same date is used in Canada, Australia, New Zealand, India and many other countries.

Mother's Day in May is one of the biggest days of the year for restaurant bookings and flower deliveries in North America, so the days remaining are a handy deadline for ordering. Note that the UK celebrates Mothering Sunday in March instead.
