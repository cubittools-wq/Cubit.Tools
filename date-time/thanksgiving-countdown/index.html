Thanksgiving Countdown (US)

Our Thanksgiving Countdown (US) shows exactly how long is left until Thanksgiving (US). The live timer counts down the days, hours, minutes and seconds to the fourth Thursday in November.

Thanksgiving in the United States is held on the fourth Thursday in November. President Abraham Lincoln proclaimed a national day of thanksgiving in 1863, and Congress fixed the date as the fourth Thursday in 1941. Families gather for a meal with roast turkey, stuffing, cranberry sauce and pumpkin pie, and the day is associated with the Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade in New York, American football games and the presidential pardoning of a turkey.
