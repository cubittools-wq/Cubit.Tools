Pancake Day Countdown

Our Pancake Day Countdown shows exactly how long is left until Pancake Day. The live timer counts down the days, hours, minutes and seconds to the Tuesday 47 days before Easter Sunday, the day before Ash Wednesday.

Pancake Day is the popular name for Shrove Tuesday, the day before Ash Wednesday and the start of Lent. Traditionally, households used up their eggs, milk and fat before the fasting season, and pancakes were the ideal way to do it. "Shrove" comes from the old English verb "shrive", meaning to confess and receive forgiveness. Pancake races, such as the one in Olney, Buckinghamshire, have been run for centuries.

Get the batter ready

The date moves with Easter, always 47 days before Easter Sunday and falling between 3 February and 9 March. A simple batter needs just flour, eggs and milk, so it is worth checking the countdown so you can stock up in advance, and decide whether you are a lemon-and-sugar or a chocolate-spread person.
