Talk Like a Pirate Day Countdown

Our Talk Like a Pirate Day Countdown shows exactly how long is left until Talk Like a Pirate Day. The live timer counts down the days, hours, minutes and seconds to 19 September.

International Talk Like a Pirate Day is a parodic holiday held every year on 19 September, when people speak in pirate slang, with "Ahoy, matey!", "Arrr!" and "Shiver me timbers!". It was invented in 1995 by two Americans, John Baur and Mark Summers, during a game of racquetball, and it spread widely after the US humorist Dave Barry wrote about it in his newspaper column in 2002.
