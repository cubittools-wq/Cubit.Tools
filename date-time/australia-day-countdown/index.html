Australia Day Countdown

Our Australia Day Countdown shows exactly how long is left until Australia Day. The live timer counts down the days, hours, minutes and seconds to 26 January.

Australia Day is celebrated on 26 January and marks the arrival of the First Fleet of British ships at Sydney Cove in 1788. It is a national public holiday, with fireworks, barbecues, community events and citizenship ceremonies. It is also a day of reflection and debate, and many Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander people mark it as Survival Day or Invasion Day. If 26 January falls on a weekend, many states observe the holiday on the following Monday.

Summer in the southern hemisphere

In Australia the day falls in the middle of summer, so many events are outdoors. If you live in the northern hemisphere, the countdown is a good way to plan an Australian-themed get-together in the depths of winter.
