Diwali Countdown

Our Diwali Countdown shows exactly how long is left until Diwali. The live timer counts down the days, hours, minutes and seconds to the new-moon night of the Hindu month of Kartika, usually in October or November.

Diwali, the Festival of Lights, is celebrated by Hindus, Sikhs, Jains and some Buddhists, and is one of the biggest festivals in the Indian calendar. The main day falls on the new-moon night in the Hindu lunar month of Ashvin or Kartika, so the date moves between mid-October and mid-November. It is a five-day festival: homes are lit with oil lamps called diyas, decorated with rangoli patterns, and families exchange sweets and gifts, often praying to Lakshmi, the goddess of wealth.

Leicester in the UK holds one of the largest Diwali celebrations outside India, with a switch-on of lights and a firework display.

Dates can differ by region

The dates shown are for the main day of Diwali, Lakshmi Puja. Regional calendars in India and overseas sometimes list the festival a day earlier or later, and the full five days begin two days before the main day with Dhanteras.
