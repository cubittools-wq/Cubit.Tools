Friday the 13th Countdown

Our Friday the 13th Countdown shows exactly how long is left until the next Friday the 13th. The live timer counts down the days, hours, minutes and seconds to the next Friday that falls on the 13th of a month.

Friday the 13th happens whenever the 13th day of a month falls on a Friday, which is at least once and up to three times in every year. Many people consider it unlucky: the fear of Friday the 13th has a name, paraskevidekatriaphobia, while the fear of the number 13 itself is triskaidekaphobia. The superstition is not old, and the horror film Friday the 13th, released in 1980, helped to popularise it further.

Over the calendar's 400-year cycle, the 13th falls on a Friday slightly more often than on any other day of the week.

Superstition and statistics

Surveys suggest that a significant number of people avoid flying, travelling or making big decisions on Friday the 13th. Whether you are superstitious or not, the countdown shows how long you have until the next one, and the list above shows the next few.
