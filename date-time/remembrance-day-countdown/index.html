Remembrance Day Countdown

Our Remembrance Day Countdown shows exactly how long is left until Remembrance Day (Armistice Day). The live timer counts down the days, hours, minutes and seconds to 11 November.

Remembrance Day, also called Armistice Day, is on 11 November and marks the end of fighting in the First World War, when the armistice with Germany took effect at 11am on 11 November 1918. It is observed in the UK and Commonwealth countries with a two-minute silence at 11am, and is a public holiday in France and Belgium. Poppies are worn in the run-up in memory of those who have died in conflict. In the United States the same date is Veterans Day.

Not to be confused with Remembrance Sunday

In the UK the national service takes place on Remembrance Sunday, the second Sunday in November, while Remembrance Day always falls on 11 November itself. Many schools, workplaces and communities hold a silence at 11am on the day.
