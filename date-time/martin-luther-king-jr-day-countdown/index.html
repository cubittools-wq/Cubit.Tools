Martin Luther King Jr. Day Countdown

Our Martin Luther King Jr. Day Countdown shows exactly how long is left until Martin Luther King Jr. Day. The live timer counts down the days, hours, minutes and seconds to the third Monday in January.

Martin Luther King Jr. Day is a United States federal holiday on the third Monday in January, close to King's birthday on 15 January. It honours the life and legacy of the civil rights leader, who was born in 1929 and assassinated in 1968. President Ronald Reagan signed the holiday into law in 1983, and it was first observed in 1986. It is also a national day of service, when many Americans volunteer in their communities.
