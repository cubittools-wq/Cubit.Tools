Anzac Day Countdown

Our Anzac Day Countdown shows exactly how long is left until Anzac Day. The live timer counts down the days, hours, minutes and seconds to 25 April.

Anzac Day on 25 April commemorates the Australian and New Zealand Army Corps, whose troops landed at Gallipoli in Turkey in 1915 during the First World War. It has grown to honour all Australians and New Zealanders who have served and died in war. The day begins with dawn services, followed by marches, and many people wear a sprig of rosemary or a poppy. Anzac biscuits, made with oats and golden syrup, are a traditional treat, and the phrase "Lest we forget" is recited.

Dawn services

Dawn services take place at the time of the original landing, so they start very early in the morning, and there are ceremonies in London, Gallipoli and other places overseas. The countdown helps you plan your attendance.
