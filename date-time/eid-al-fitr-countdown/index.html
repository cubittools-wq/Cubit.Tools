Eid al-Fitr Countdown

Our Eid al-Fitr Countdown shows exactly how long is left until Eid al-Fitr. The live timer counts down the days, hours, minutes and seconds to the first day of Shawwal, the day after Ramadan ends.

Eid al-Fitr, meaning "festival of breaking the fast", marks the end of Ramadan and is one of the two major festivals of the Islamic year. It falls on the first day of Shawwal, the tenth month of the Islamic calendar. Muslims gather for a special morning prayer, wear new or their best clothes, give charity known as Zakat al-Fitr, and share meals and gifts with family and friends. The greeting "Eid Mubarak" means "blessed Eid".

Date depends on the moon

Islamic dates follow the Umm al-Qura calendar used in Saudi Arabia. Because each month begins with the sighting of the new moon, mosques and communities in other countries, including the UK, may begin a day later, so please check locally. Eid is often confirmed only the evening before.
