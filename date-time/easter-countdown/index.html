Easter Countdown

Our Easter Countdown shows exactly how long is left until Easter. The live timer counts down the days, hours, minutes and seconds to the first Sunday after the first full moon on or after 21 March.

Easter Sunday celebrates the resurrection of Jesus in the Christian faith and is the most important date in the church calendar. It is a moveable feast: in Western churches it falls on the first Sunday after the ecclesiastical full moon on or after 21 March, so it can land anywhere between 22 March and 25 April. The Eastern Orthodox churches calculate their own date using the Julian calendar, which is often later.

Planning the Easter weekend

In the UK the Easter weekend is a four-day break, from Good Friday to Easter Monday, which makes it a key deadline for travel bookings, food orders and egg hunts. The date changes every year, so check the countdown before you plan.
