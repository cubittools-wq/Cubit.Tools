Mid-Autumn Festival Countdown

Our Mid-Autumn Festival Countdown shows exactly how long is left until the Mid-Autumn Festival. The live timer counts down the days, hours, minutes and seconds to the 15th day of the eighth lunar month.

The Mid-Autumn Festival, also known as the Moon Festival, falls on the 15th day of the eighth month of the Chinese lunar calendar, when the moon is at its fullest and brightest. Families gather to admire the moon, light lanterns and share mooncakes, dense pastries filled with lotus seed paste or salted egg yolk. It is a public holiday in mainland China and Taiwan, it is celebrated as Tet Trung Thu in Vietnam, and Korea's Chuseok harvest festival falls on the same day.

A festival of reunion

The festival is above all a time for reunion, and people travel long distances to be with family. If you are sending mooncakes or gifts, the days remaining are a useful deadline for orders and shipping, since gift boxes are in high demand in the weeks beforehand.
