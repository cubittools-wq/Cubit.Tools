Ramadan Countdown

Our Ramadan Countdown shows exactly how long is left until Ramadan. The live timer counts down the days, hours, minutes and seconds to the ninth month of the Islamic lunar calendar.

Ramadan is the ninth month of the Islamic calendar, during which Muslims fast from dawn to sunset, and one of the five pillars of Islam. It is a time of prayer, reflection, charity and community, with families and friends gathering to break the fast at sunset with an evening meal called iftar. The month ends with the festival of Eid al-Fitr.

The Islamic calendar follows the moon and is about ten or eleven days shorter than the solar year, so Ramadan begins around ten or eleven days earlier each year and moves through every season over about 33 years.

Check the start date locally

Islamic dates follow the Umm al-Qura calendar used in Saudi Arabia. Because each month begins with the sighting of the new moon, mosques and communities in other countries, including the UK, may begin a day later, so please check locally. The dates shown are for the first day of Ramadan.
