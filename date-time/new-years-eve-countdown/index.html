New Year's Eve Countdown

Our New Year's Eve Countdown shows exactly how long is left until New Year's Eve. The live timer counts down the days, hours, minutes and seconds to 31 December.

New Year's Eve is 31 December, the last day of the year in the Gregorian calendar. Celebrations build towards midnight with fireworks, parties and the singing of Auld Lang Syne. Sydney is one of the first major cities to welcome the new year, thanks to its Harbour Bridge fireworks, while London, New York and Edinburgh each have their own famous midnight moments.

How Many Days Until New Year's Eve?

Whichever day you visit, the countdown above shows how much time remains. As soon as the day has passed, it moves on to the next one automatically. New Year's Eve falls on 31 December.
