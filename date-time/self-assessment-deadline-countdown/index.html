Self Assessment Deadline Countdown

Our Self Assessment Deadline Countdown shows exactly how long is left until the Self Assessment deadline. The live timer counts down the days, hours, minutes and seconds to 11:59pm on 31 January.

The Self Assessment deadline is the date by which UK taxpayers must file their online tax return and pay any tax owed for the previous tax year, which ends on 5 April. The deadline is 31 January, with a paper return due earlier, on 31 October. HMRC usually charges an automatic penalty of Â£100 for a late online return, even if no tax is due, with further penalties and interest if the return and payment are later still.

Don't leave it to the last minute

HMRC's systems are at their busiest in the final week, and you need to register for Self Assessment well ahead of the deadline, since the activation code takes time to arrive by post. This is general information rather than tax advice, so check GOV.UK for the current rules for your circumstances.
