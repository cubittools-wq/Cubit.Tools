Summer Countdown

Our Summer Countdown shows exactly how long is left until Summer. The live timer counts down the days, hours, minutes and seconds to 1 June, the start of meteorological summer.

Meteorological summer begins on 1 June and runs until 31 August, the warmest three months of the year in the northern hemisphere. The astronomical start of summer is the June solstice, around 21 June, which brings the longest day. Weather services such as the Met Office use the meteorological seasons for their records. Dates and seasons given are for the northern hemisphere. In the southern hemisphere the seasons are reversed, so the same date marks the start of the opposite season.
