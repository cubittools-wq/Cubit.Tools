Graduation Countdown

Counting down to graduation

Enter the date and time of your ceremony above and the timer shows how long is left until you walk across the stage. Because the counter also shows the weeks remaining, you can use it to pace your final assignments and dissertation.

What to sort before the day

Common tasks include booking your cap and gown, ordering guest tickets, arranging photographs, booking travel and accommodation for family, and choosing an outfit. Many universities have deadlines for gown hire and guest tickets a month or more before the ceremony.

For school and college too

The same countdown works for school leavers' proms and end-of-term celebrations: just enter the date. Whatever you enter stays in your own browser, so the countdown is still there the next time you visit this page on the same device, and nothing is sent to us.
