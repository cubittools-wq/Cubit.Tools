Super Bowl LXI Countdown

Our Super Bowl LXI Countdown shows exactly how long is left until Super Bowl LXI. The live timer counts down the days, hours, minutes and seconds to Sunday 14 February 2027.

Super Bowl LXI, the 61st Super Bowl and the NFL championship game, is scheduled for 14 February 2027 at SoFi Stadium in Inglewood, California. It will be the first Super Bowl played on both Valentine's Day and Presidents' Day weekend. The game is the most-watched television event in the United States each year, with a halftime show and famously expensive commercials.

How Many Days Until Super Bowl LXI?

Whichever day you visit, the countdown above shows how much time remains. The countdown above ends on Sunday 14 February 2027.

Watching from the UK

The Super Bowl kicks off in the evening in the US, which means the early hours of Monday morning in the UK. The countdown is to the start of the day, 14 February, so check the official kick-off time nearer the time.
