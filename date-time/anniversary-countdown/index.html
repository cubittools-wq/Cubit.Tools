Anniversary Countdown

How long until your anniversary?

Enter the date of your wedding, engagement or the day you got together and the countdown shows how long is left until the next anniversary, which anniversary it will be, and the days, hours, minutes and seconds remaining. If the anniversary is today, it shows a message instead.

Traditional gifts by year

Each wedding anniversary has a traditional gift theme: paper for the first, cotton for the second, leather for the third, and so on, through silver for the 25th, pearl for the 30th, ruby for the 40th, gold for the 50th and diamond for the 60th. The countdown is a handy reminder to buy the right card and gift.

Never miss the date

Book the restaurant, order flowers or plan a surprise in good time by keeping an eye on the number of days left. Whatever you enter stays in your own browser, so the countdown is still there the next time you visit this page on the same device, and nothing is sent to us.
