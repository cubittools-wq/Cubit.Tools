How the countdown calculator works

Pick a date and a time in the box above and the counter starts straight away, showing the days, hours, minutes and seconds left until that moment. It updates every second, and it also gives you the time in weeks and days, or in years, months and days for long countdowns, so that you can see it at a glance.

What can you count down to?

Anything with a date and time: a holiday, a wedding, a product launch, a deadline, an exam, a flight, a party or the end of the working week. If you want a countdown to a fixed event, such as Christmas or Easter, our ready-made countdowns have the date built in.

Your countdown is saved

Whatever you enter stays in your own browser, so the countdown is still there the next time you visit this page on the same device, and nothing is sent to us. If you would like several countdowns at once, use a different page for each, such as the wedding, holiday or deadline countdown.
