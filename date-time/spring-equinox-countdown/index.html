Spring Equinox Countdown

Our Spring Equinox Countdown shows exactly how long is left until the March equinox. The live timer counts down the days, hours, minutes and seconds to the moment of the March equinox.

The March equinox is the moment when the sun crosses the celestial equator heading north, and it marks the astronomical start of spring in the northern hemisphere and of autumn in the southern hemisphere. On the day of the equinox, day and night are close to equal in length everywhere on Earth. It falls on 19, 20 or 21 March, depending on the year, and is the moment counted down to here, shown to the nearest minute.

Many cultures mark the date, including Nowruz, the Persian New Year, and the spring equinox celebrations of many pagan traditions.

How Many Days Until the Spring Equinox?

Whichever day you visit, the countdown above shows how much time remains. The countdown ends at the moment of the March equinox.

Equinox and the seasons

The times are calculated in UTC and shown in your local time on the countdown. The meteorological start of spring is 1 March, which is a different date from the astronomical start on the equinox. Dates and seasons given are for the northern hemisphere. In the southern hemisphere the seasons are reversed, so the same date marks the start of the opposite season.
