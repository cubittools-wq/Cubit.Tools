Wimbledon 2027 Countdown

Our Wimbledon 2027 Countdown shows exactly how long is left until Wimbledon 2027. The live timer counts down the days, hours, minutes and seconds to Monday 28 June 2027, the first day of play.

Wimbledon 2027 runs from Monday 28 June to Sunday 11 July 2027 at the All England Club in London. It is the oldest tennis tournament in the world, first held in 1877, and the only Grand Slam still played on grass. Traditions include an all-white dress code for players, strawberries and cream, and the queue for tickets. Centre Court and No. 1 Court have retractable roofs, so rain no longer stops play there.
