Father's Day Countdown

Our Father's Day Countdown shows exactly how long is left until Father's Day. The live timer counts down the days, hours, minutes and seconds to the third Sunday in June.

Father's Day is celebrated on the third Sunday in June in the UK, the United States, Canada and many other countries. The first Father's Day is usually credited to Sonora Smart Dodd of Spokane, Washington, who organised a celebration in 1910 in honour of her father. It was recognised by presidential proclamation in 1966 and made a permanent national holiday in the United States in 1972.
