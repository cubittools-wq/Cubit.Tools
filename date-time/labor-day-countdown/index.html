Labor Day Countdown

Our Labor Day Countdown shows exactly how long is left until Labor Day. The live timer counts down the days, hours, minutes and seconds to the first Monday in September.

Labor Day is a United States federal holiday on the first Monday in September, honouring the contributions of American workers and the labour movement. The first Labor Day parade was held in New York City in 1882, and Congress made it a federal holiday in 1894. It is regarded as the unofficial end of summer, with barbecues, parades and sales, and traditionally marks the start of the school year and the football season.
