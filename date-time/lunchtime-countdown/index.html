Lunchtime Countdown

How long until lunch?

Set the time your lunch break starts and the countdown shows the hours, minutes and seconds until then. When the time passes, it starts counting down to the same time tomorrow, so you can leave it open all week.

A better lunch break

Taking a proper break away from your desk is linked to better concentration in the afternoon. Use the countdown to plan the morning, to schedule meetings so that they end before lunch, and to decide when to leave to beat the queue.

Your time is remembered

Whatever you enter stays in your own browser, so the countdown is still there the next time you visit this page on the same device, and nothing is sent to us.
