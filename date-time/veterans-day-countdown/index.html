Veterans Day Countdown

Our Veterans Day Countdown shows exactly how long is left until Veterans Day. The live timer counts down the days, hours, minutes and seconds to 11 November.

Veterans Day is a United States federal holiday on 11 November honouring all military veterans who have served in the US armed forces. It falls on the anniversary of the armistice that ended the First World War in 1918, and was originally called Armistice Day until Congress renamed it in 1954. Parades, ceremonies and a national ceremony at Arlington National Cemetery mark the day, which is different from Memorial Day, which honours those who died in service.

Veterans Day and Memorial Day

Veterans Day honours all those who have served, living or dead, while Memorial Day honours those who died in service. Many restaurants and businesses offer discounts and free meals to veterans on the day.
