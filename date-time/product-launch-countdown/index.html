Product Launch Countdown

Countdown to go-live

Enter the date and time your product, sale, website or campaign goes live, and the counter shows how long the team has left. Keep it open on a shared screen so that everyone can see how much time remains for testing, content and approvals.

A launch checklist

In the last weeks before a launch, freeze the product data and prices, test the checkout and payment methods, prepare customer service answers, schedule emails and social posts, check stock and delivery cut-offs, and plan the first hours of monitoring. Leave a buffer for last-minute fixes.

Launch time zones

The timer uses the time zone of your device. If a launch is timed for a different region, convert the go-live time before you enter it. Whatever you enter stays in your own browser, so the countdown is still there the next time you visit this page on the same device, and nothing is sent to us.
