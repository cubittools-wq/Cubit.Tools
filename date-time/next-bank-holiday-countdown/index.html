Next Bank Holiday Countdown

Our Next Bank Holiday Countdown shows exactly how long is left until the next bank holiday. The live timer counts down the days, hours, minutes and seconds to the next bank holiday in England and Wales.

Bank holidays are public holidays in the UK, when most banks, schools and many businesses close. England and Wales have eight a year: New Year's Day, Good Friday, Easter Monday, the Early May bank holiday, the Spring bank holiday, the Summer bank holiday, Christmas Day and Boxing Day. Scotland and Northern Ireland have their own additional holidays, such as 2 January and St Andrew's Day in Scotland and St Patrick's Day and the Battle of the Boyne in Northern Ireland.

This countdown always shows the next bank holiday in England and Wales, with its name. When New Year's Day, Christmas Day or Boxing Day falls on a weekend, a substitute weekday is given as a bank holiday, and the date shown is that substitute day.

Plan your long weekends

Many bank holidays fall on a Monday, creating a long weekend, and the Easter weekend runs from Good Friday to Easter Monday. Use the countdown to see how long you have to book time off around them. Dates are set by the UK government, which can add extra holidays for special occasions, so always check GOV.UK for the latest list.
