Daylight Saving Time Starts Countdown (US)

Our Daylight Saving Time Starts Countdown (US) shows exactly how long is left until Daylight Saving Time starting. The live timer counts down the days, hours, minutes and seconds to 2am on the second Sunday in March.

In the United States and Canada, Daylight Saving Time begins at 2am local time on the second Sunday in March, when clocks are moved forward by one hour. The change means one less hour of sleep, but longer evenings. Under the Energy Policy Act of 2005, in effect since 2007, DST runs for about eight months, from March to November. Most of Arizona and Hawaii do not change their clocks, and neither do several other US territories.

When Does Daylight Saving Time Start?

Whichever day you visit, the countdown above shows how much time remains. As soon as the day has passed, it moves on to the next one automatically. The countdown ends at 2am on the second Sunday in March.

Spring forward

The countdown is to 2am on your device's local time, although some devices show the target as 3am, because 2am is skipped on that morning. Most phones and computers update automatically, but you will need to change ovens, cars and wall clocks by hand. The day is also a good moment to check the batteries in smoke alarms, a tip many fire services repeat.
