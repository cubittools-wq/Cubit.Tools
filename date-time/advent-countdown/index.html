Advent Countdown

Our Advent Countdown shows exactly how long is left until Advent. The live timer counts down the days, hours, minutes and seconds to the Sunday between 27 November and 3 December, four Sundays before Christmas.

Advent is the season of preparation before Christmas in the Western Christian calendar, and Advent Sunday marks the start of the church year. It begins on the fourth Sunday before Christmas Day, so it always falls between 27 November and 3 December. Many churches and homes light one candle on an Advent wreath for each Sunday, and the season runs until Christmas Eve.

Advent Sunday and advent calendars

Because Advent Sunday moves around, the season is not always exactly 24 days long, which is why most advent calendars simply begin on 1 December. If you are counting down to the first door on a calendar, use our Advent Calendar Countdown, which counts to 1 December.
