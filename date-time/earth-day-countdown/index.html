Earth Day Countdown

Our Earth Day Countdown shows exactly how long is left until Earth Day. The live timer counts down the days, hours, minutes and seconds to 22 April.

Earth Day is held every year on 22 April to show support for environmental protection. The first Earth Day took place in 1970, organised by the US senator Gaylord Nelson, and helped to inspire the creation of the US Environmental Protection Agency and landmark environmental laws. It is now celebrated in more than 190 countries, with tree planting, clean-ups, marches and educational events.

How Many Days Until Earth Day?

Whichever day you visit, the countdown above shows how much time remains. As soon as the day has passed, it moves on to the next one automatically. Earth Day falls on 22 April.

Ways to take part

Simple actions, such as planting a tree, organising a litter pick, switching to a green energy supplier or cutting down on single-use plastic, are typical of the day. Use the countdown to plan an event for your school, workplace or community.
