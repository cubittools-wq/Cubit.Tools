Pentecost Countdown

Our Pentecost Countdown shows exactly how long is left until Pentecost (Whit Sunday). The live timer counts down the days, hours, minutes and seconds to the seventh Sunday after Easter Sunday, 49 days later.

Pentecost, also known as Whit Sunday in Britain, is the seventh Sunday after Easter and celebrates the coming of the Holy Spirit to the disciples. It is often called the birthday of the Christian church. In Britain Whit Monday was a bank holiday until 1971, when it was replaced by the Spring bank holiday on the last Monday in May.

Fifty days after Easter

The name Pentecost comes from the Greek for "fiftieth", because it falls fifty days after Easter when counting Easter Sunday itself. The date therefore moves every year, between 10 May and 13 June.
