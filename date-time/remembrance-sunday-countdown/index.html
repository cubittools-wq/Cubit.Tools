Remembrance Sunday Countdown

Our Remembrance Sunday Countdown shows exactly how long is left until Remembrance Sunday. The live timer counts down the days, hours, minutes and seconds to the second Sunday in November.

Remembrance Sunday is held on the second Sunday in November, the Sunday closest to Armistice Day on 11 November. It honours the members of the armed forces who died in the two world wars and later conflicts. The national service takes place at the Cenotaph in Whitehall, London, with a two-minute silence at 11am, wreath laying by the royal family and politicians, and a march past of veterans. Poppies, sold by the Royal British Legion, are worn in the weeks before.

The two-minute silence

The two-minute silence on Remembrance Sunday begins at 11am, and communities across the UK gather at war memorials to observe it. If you are planning to attend, arrive in good time, as local services and parades often begin early.
