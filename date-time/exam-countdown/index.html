Exam Countdown

Make every day count

Enter the date and time of your exam and the counter shows exactly how long you have left to prepare. Seeing the number of days shrink can make it easier to divide your revision into manageable chunks, and the line under the timer gives you the number of full weeks left.

Building a revision plan

A simple approach is to count the days until the exam, subtract a few for rest and buffer, then divide the remaining days between your subjects or topics. Leave the last few days for practice papers and light revision rather than new material, and keep some time in each week for sleep, exercise and a break.

Several exams?

If you have more than one exam, you can keep one countdown per subject by using the main countdown calculator on another browser or device. Whatever you enter stays in your own browser, so the countdown is still there the next time you visit this page on the same device, and nothing is sent to us.
