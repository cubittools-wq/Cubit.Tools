Holiday Countdown

How long until your holiday?

Enter the date and time of your departure above, such as the time of your flight or the day you set off, and the countdown shows exactly how long is left. It also shows the weeks and days, and, if you like counting like a child, you can use our sleeps countdown for the number of nights.

Holiday planning milestones

The days remaining help you plan the practical side of a trip: passport renewals and visas, travel insurance, booking airport parking, ordering currency, checking your luggage allowance, and packing. A common rule is to check your passport is valid at least three months beyond your return date, and some countries need six.

Vacation or holiday

British English uses holiday and American English uses vacation, but the countdown is the same. Whatever you enter stays in your own browser, so the countdown is still there the next time you visit this page on the same device, and nothing is sent to us.
