Sleeps Countdown

How many sleeps to go?

Young children find it hard to think in days, weeks or months, but they understand sleeps: each night's sleep is one step closer to a birthday party, a trip, a visit from grandparents or the first day at school. Choose the date above and the counter shows the number of sleeps left, as well as the days, hours, minutes and seconds.

Make it a game

Draw a chart with one box for each sleep and let the child tick one off every morning, or make a paper chain and remove a link every day. Read the number on the counter aloud and check that it matches the chain.

Counting sleeps

A sleep is counted for every night before the day, so the number of sleeps is the number of days rounded up. For Christmas, use our Sleeps Until Christmas Countdown. Whatever you enter stays in your own browser, so the countdown is still there the next time you visit this page on the same device, and nothing is sent to us.
