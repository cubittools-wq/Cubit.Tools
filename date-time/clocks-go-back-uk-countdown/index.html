Clocks Go Back Countdown (UK)

Our Clocks Go Back Countdown (UK) shows exactly how long is left until the clocks going back. The live timer counts down the days, hours, minutes and seconds to 2am on the last Sunday in October, when BST ends.

In the UK the clocks go back by one hour at 2am British Summer Time, which is 1am Greenwich Mean Time, on the last Sunday in October. That night is an hour longer, giving an extra hour in bed, but the evenings become darker earlier. The change returns the country to GMT for the winter, and the days are shorter until the December solstice.

The date is set by law, and the same weekend applies across the European Union. The UK and the EU have both debated ending the twice-yearly change, but it remains in place.

When Do the Clocks Go Back?

Whichever day you visit, the countdown above shows how much time remains. The countdown ends at 2am on the last Sunday in October, when BST ends.

An extra hour, shorter evenings

The clocks change at 1am GMT, which is 1am UTC, and the countdown shows the time in your own time zone. The same dates apply across the European Union. The target time shown on the countdown is the new clock time straight after the change, so in the UK it reads 1am GMT. If you work night shifts, are travelling or have anything scheduled in the small hours, check your times before the change happens.
