Small Business Saturday Countdown (UK)

Our Small Business Saturday Countdown (UK) shows exactly how long is left until Small Business Saturday (UK). The live timer counts down the days, hours, minutes and seconds to the first Saturday in December.

Small Business Saturday UK takes place on the first Saturday of December and encourages people to shop and support small, independent businesses. The campaign was launched in the UK in 2013, following the American Small Business Saturday held in November each year. It is supported by small business owners, councils and organisations across the country, and many use the day to run events, offers and window displays.
