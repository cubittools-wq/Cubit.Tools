US Election Day Countdown

Our US Election Day Countdown shows exactly how long is left until US Election Day. The live timer counts down the days, hours, minutes and seconds to the Tuesday after the first Monday in November.

Election Day in the United States is held on the Tuesday after the first Monday in November, a date set by Congress in 1845. Federal elections take place every two years: presidential elections every four years, together with the whole House of Representatives and about a third of the Senate, and midterm elections halfway through a president's term. State and local races are often decided on the same day.
