International Women's Day Countdown

Our International Women's Day Countdown shows exactly how long is left until International Women's Day. The live timer counts down the days, hours, minutes and seconds to 8 March.

International Women's Day is celebrated on 8 March each year to recognise the achievements of women and to call for gender equality. Its roots lie in the labour and suffrage movements of the early twentieth century, and the United Nations began observing the day in 1975. It is a public holiday in many countries, and events, marches and campaigns are held around the world, with a different theme each year.
