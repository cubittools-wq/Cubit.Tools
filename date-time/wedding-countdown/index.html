Wedding Countdown

Counting down to the big day

Enter the date and the time of your ceremony and the countdown ticks down to the moment you say your vows. It also shows the time in weeks and days, or in months, which is how most wedding planners break down their timelines.

A typical wedding timeline

Many couples plan to book the venue 12 to 18 months ahead, send save-the-dates around 6 to 9 months out, send invitations 2 to 3 months ahead, and finalise the seating plan and suppliers in the last month. Check your figures against the number of weeks left on the counter to see whether you are on track.

Share the excitement

You can keep the countdown open on a tablet or screen at home and check in on it as the day nears. Whatever you enter stays in your own browser, so the countdown is still there the next time you visit this page on the same device, and nothing is sent to us.
