Cyber Monday Countdown

Our Cyber Monday Countdown shows exactly how long is left until Cyber Monday. The live timer counts down the days, hours, minutes and seconds to the Monday after Thanksgiving weekend.

Cyber Monday is the Monday after US Thanksgiving weekend and a major day for online shopping. The term was coined in 2005 by Shop.org, part of the National Retail Federation, after noticing that shoppers who had been in stores over the weekend went online on Monday. It has grown into one of the biggest online shopping days of the year in the United States and the UK.

How Many Days Until Cyber Monday?

Whichever day you visit, the countdown above shows how much time remains. As soon as the day has passed, it moves on to the next one automatically. Cyber Monday falls on the Monday after Thanksgiving weekend.

Online deals and delivery deadlines

Cyber Monday is aimed at online retailers, particularly technology, and many sellers now run week-long sales. For sellers, the countdown is a reminder to have campaigns, stock and customer service ready, and for shoppers, it helps to plan which deals to target.
