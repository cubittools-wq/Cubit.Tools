Holi Countdown

Our Holi Countdown shows exactly how long is left until Holi. The live timer counts down the days, hours, minutes and seconds to the day after the full moon of the Hindu month of Phalguna, usually in March.

Holi, the festival of colours, welcomes the arrival of spring and celebrates the victory of good over evil. It falls on the day after the full moon in the Hindu month of Phalguna, so the date changes each year, usually in March. The evening before, communities light bonfires in the Holika Dahan ceremony. On the main day people throw coloured powder and water at each other, share sweets and drinks, and visit friends and family.

Holi is celebrated across India and Nepal and by Hindu communities around the world, and colour-run and festival events are held in cities across the UK and the United States.

Which day is Holi?

Holi is usually a two-day festival: Holika Dahan on the evening of the full moon, and Rangwali Holi, the day of colours, the next day. The countdown above is to the day of colours. Some regions and calendars differ by a day, so check with your local community if you are planning an event.
