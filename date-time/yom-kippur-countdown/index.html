Yom Kippur Countdown

Our Yom Kippur Countdown shows exactly how long is left until Yom Kippur. The live timer counts down the days, hours, minutes and seconds to the tenth day of Tishrei, beginning at sunset the evening before.

Yom Kippur, the Day of Atonement, is the holiest day of the Jewish year. It falls on the tenth day of Tishrei, ten days after Rosh Hashanah, and is observed with a fast of about twenty-five hours, prayer, and time in the synagogue. Many people wear white and avoid work, eating, drinking, bathing and wearing leather shoes. The day closes with the sounding of the shofar.
