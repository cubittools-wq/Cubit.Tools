Pregnancy Due Date Countdown

Counting down to your due date

If you know your due date, enter it above. If you only know the first day of your last period, enter that instead and the due date is worked out for you, and the other way round. Whichever box you edited last is the one that is used. The counter shows the time left to your due date and how many weeks and days pregnant you are.

How the due date is worked out

A full-term pregnancy is about 40 weeks, or 280 days, from the first day of the last menstrual period, the rule used by doctors and midwives. It assumes a cycle of about 28 days, so the date is an estimate. Only around 5% of babies arrive on their due date, and it is normal for babies to arrive in the weeks either side.

Your midwife's date

The date given after your dating scan is the one to use, because scans are more accurate than dates from your last period. This tool is not medical advice, so speak to your midwife or doctor about your pregnancy. Whatever you enter stays in your own browser, so the countdown is still there the next time you visit this page on the same device, and nothing is sent to us.
