Spring Countdown

Our Spring Countdown shows exactly how long is left until Spring. The live timer counts down the days, hours, minutes and seconds to 1 March, the start of meteorological spring.

Meteorological spring begins on 1 March and runs until 31 May. Meteorologists, including the UK's Met Office, divide the year into four three-month seasons, which makes it easier to compare weather statistics from year to year. The astronomical start of spring, the March equinox, falls around 20 March, so the two dates differ by about three weeks. Dates and seasons given are for the northern hemisphere. In the southern hemisphere the seasons are reversed, so the same date marks the start of the opposite season.

Two ways to count spring

This countdown is to the meteorological start of spring on 1 March. If you would rather count down to the March equinox, when day and night are almost equal in length, use our Spring Equinox Countdown. Many people also think of spring as the point when the clocks go forward and the evenings get lighter.
