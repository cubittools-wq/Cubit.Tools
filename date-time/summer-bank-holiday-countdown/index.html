Summer Bank Holiday Countdown

Our Summer Bank Holiday Countdown shows exactly how long is left until the Summer bank holiday. The live timer counts down the days, hours, minutes and seconds to the last Monday in August.

The Summer bank holiday is on the last Monday in August in England, Wales and Northern Ireland, and is the last bank holiday of the year before Christmas. In Scotland the equivalent summer bank holiday is on the first Monday in August. It is often the last long weekend of the school summer holidays, and events such as Notting Hill Carnival in London and Reading and Leeds festivals take place around it.
