Moving Day Countdown

Counting down to moving day

Choose the date you are getting the keys or moving out, and the countdown shows how long you have left. The line beneath the timer shows the number of weeks and days, which is a useful way to plan a move, as most moving checklists are laid out in weeks.

A moving checklist by weeks

Around eight weeks before, book removals and start decluttering. Around four weeks before, start packing rooms you rarely use and arrange to forward your post. In the last two weeks, notify banks, utility providers, the doctor and the DVLA of your new address, and pack a box of essentials for the first night.

Moving abroad or renting

The same counter works for the end of a tenancy, when you may need to give the landlord notice, arrange a final meter reading and clean the property. Whatever you enter stays in your own browser, so the countdown is still there the next time you visit this page on the same device, and nothing is sent to us.
