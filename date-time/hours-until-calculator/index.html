Hours Until Calculator

Counting down hours

For short countdowns, the hours matter most. Enter the date and time of your event above and the timer shows the days, hours, minutes and seconds left. For a countdown of under 24 hours, the days simply show zero, and the hours and minutes take the lead.

Good for

Departure times, train and flight check-ins, ticket releases, online sales that start at a set time, kick-offs, meetings and deadlines. Choose the exact time so that the countdown reaches zero at the right moment.

Time zones

The countdown uses the time zone of your device. If the event is in another time zone, convert its start time to your local time before you enter it. Whatever you enter stays in your own browser, so the countdown is still there the next time you visit this page on the same device, and nothing is sent to us.
