Advent Calendar Countdown

Our Advent Calendar Countdown shows exactly how long is left until the first advent calendar door. The live timer counts down the days, hours, minutes and seconds to 1 December.

Most advent calendars start on 1 December, with a door or pocket to open each day until Christmas Eve, although some run to Christmas Day. Counting down to Christmas with a calendar developed in Germany in the nineteenth century, and printed calendars became popular in the early 1900s. Today they come filled with chocolate, tea, beauty products, toys, cheese and more.

How Many Days Until the First Advent Calendar Door?

Whichever day you visit, the countdown above shows how much time remains. As soon as the day has passed, it moves on to the next one automatically. The first advent calendar door falls on 1 December.

Get your calendar in time

The most popular advent calendars, especially the beauty and food ones, often sell out weeks before December. The days remaining are a handy reminder to order yours, and to check that any calendar you are giving as a gift arrives in time.
