Islamic New Year Countdown

Our Islamic New Year Countdown shows exactly how long is left until the Islamic New Year. The live timer counts down the days, hours, minutes and seconds to the first day of Muharram.

The Islamic New Year, or Hijri New Year, falls on the first day of Muharram, the first month of the Islamic calendar. The Hijri calendar counts years from the Hijra, the migration of the Prophet Muhammad and his followers from Mecca to Medina in 622 CE, and each year is written with the letters AH. It is a time of reflection rather than a major celebration in most communities, and the tenth day of Muharram, Ashura, is observed with fasting by many Muslims.

Why the date moves

The Islamic calendar has about 354 days, so the new year arrives roughly eleven days earlier each year in the Gregorian calendar. It moves gradually through all seasons. Islamic dates follow the Umm al-Qura calendar used in Saudi Arabia. Because each month begins with the sighting of the new moon, mosques and communities in other countries, including the UK, may begin a day later, so please check locally.
