Understanding Refrigerator Energy Ratings

Because refrigerators and freezers must run continuously 24 hours a day, 365 days a year, their energy efficiency rating has a massive impact on your household electricity bill. Modern rescaled energy labels range from class A (most efficient) down to class G (least efficient).

How Costs Are Calculated

The energy label lists total annual consumption in kilowatt-hours (kWh/annum). Multiplying this figure by your local electricity utility rate per kWh reveals your exact annual running cost.
