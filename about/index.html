About Cubit.tools

Fast, private, and simple web utilities built for everyday tasks.

Welcome to Cubit.tools. Our mission is to provide light, ultra-fast online utilities that run directly inside your web browser without unnecessary bloat or hidden fees.

Privacy-First Design

Unlike traditional web apps that send your inputs over the internet to remote servers, our tools handle computations locally in your browser wherever possible. Whether you are generating a custom passphrase or calculating financial margins, your data remains on your device.

Free & Accessible

We believe everyday tools should be simple to access and free to use. We design all of our utilities to be responsive across desktop and mobile devices with zero login requirements.
