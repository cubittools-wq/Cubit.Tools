Weight and Mass Conversion

Accurate weight conversion is crucial for shipping, cooking, fitness tracking, and scientific calculations. Easily convert between standard metric units like kilograms and grams or imperial units like pounds, ounces, and stones.

Common Weight Conversion Rates

  • 1 Kilogram = 2.20462 Pounds
  • 1 Pound = 16 Ounces
  • 1 Stone = 14 Pounds (6.35029 Kilograms)

How to convert kilograms to stones and pounds

Body weight in the UK is often given in stones and pounds. To convert from kilograms, multiply by 2.20462 to get pounds, then divide by 14. The whole number is the stones and the remainder is the pounds. For example, 70 kg is 154.3 lb, which is almost exactly 11 stone.

About the units

  • Kilograms: The base unit of mass in the metric system. Used for body weight, groceries and luggage in most of the world.
  • Grams: One thousandth of a kilogram. Used for food ingredients, post and small items.
  • Milligrams: One thousandth of a gram. Used for medicine doses and nutrition labels.
  • Tonnes: 1,000 kilograms. Used for vehicles, freight and building materials.
  • Pounds: The main imperial unit of weight, written lb. There are 14 pounds in a stone.
  • Ounces: One sixteenth of a pound, written oz. Used in recipes and for small parcels.
  • Stones: 14 pounds. Used in the UK and Ireland for body weight.

Frequently asked questions

Is weight the same as mass?

In everyday use, yes. Strictly, mass is the amount of matter in an object and weight is the force of gravity on it, but scales are set up to show mass.

Is a tonne the same as a ton?

Not quite. A metric tonne is 1,000 kg. The UK long ton is 1,016 kg and the US short ton is 907 kg.
