About the units

  • Square metres: The standard metric unit for rooms, flooring and property.
  • Square feet: Used for property in the UK and the United States.
  • Square yards: 9 square feet. Still used for carpet and fabric.
  • Acres: 4,047 square metres. Used for land in the UK and the United States.
  • Hectares: 10,000 square metres, or about 2.47 acres. Used for farmland and forestry.
  • Square kilometres: 100 hectares. Used for towns, lakes and countries.
  • Square miles: 640 acres.
