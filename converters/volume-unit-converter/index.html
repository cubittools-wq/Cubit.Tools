UK and US gallons are different

The UK (imperial) gallon and the US gallon share a name but not a size. A UK gallon is 4.546 litres and a US gallon is 3.785 litres. The same goes for pints. Check which one a figure refers to before you convert, especially for fuel economy, recipes and tank sizes.

About the units

  • Litres: The everyday metric unit of volume.
  • UK gallons: 4.546 litres, or 8 UK pints. Also called the imperial gallon.
  • UK pints: 568 ml. Still used for milk and draught beer in the UK.
  • US gallons: 3.785 litres, about 17% smaller than a UK gallon.
  • US pints: 473 ml.
  • Cubic metres: 1,000 litres. Used for water bills, concrete and gas.
  • Cubic feet: 28.3 litres. Used for fridge and freezer capacity and for shipping.
