Why MPG and L/100km Don't Convert Like Other Units

Unlike most unit conversions, MPG and L/100km have an inverse relationship rather than a simple multiplication. A higher MPG figure means better fuel economy, while a lower L/100km figure means better fuel economy. Our calculator automatically handles this inverse relationship for you.

UK mpg and US mpg

A UK gallon is 4.546 litres and a US gallon is 3.785 litres, so a car that does 50 miles to the UK gallon does only 41.6 miles to the US gallon. When you read a fuel economy figure from an American website or review, convert it before comparing it with British figures. To convert UK mpg to L/100km, divide 282.48 by the mpg figure.

About the units

  • UK mpg: Miles per UK gallon. The figure quoted for cars sold in Britain. Higher is better.
  • US mpg: Miles per US gallon. Because the US gallon is smaller, the same car has a lower US mpg figure.
  • L/100km: Litres used to travel 100 kilometres. The standard measure in Europe. Lower is better.
