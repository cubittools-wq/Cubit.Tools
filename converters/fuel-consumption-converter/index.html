Why MPG and L/100km Don't Convert Like Other Units

Unlike most unit conversions, MPG and L/100km have an inverse relationship rather than a simple multiplication. A higher MPG figure means better fuel economy, while a lower L/100km figure means better fuel economy. Our calculator automatically handles this inverse relationship for you.

How to Convert Fuel Economy Figures

To convert a fuel consumption value, enter the amount into the calculator and select whether you are converting from MPG to L/100km or from L/100km to MPG. The Fuel Consumption Converter calculates the equivalent figure instantly.

For example, 30 MPG (US) is approximately 7.84 L/100km, while 40 MPG (US) is approximately 5.88 L/100km.
