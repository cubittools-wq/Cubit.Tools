Why a 1 TB drive shows less than 1 TB

There are two ways of counting. Drive makers use decimal units, where 1 TB is 1,000,000,000,000 bytes. Many operating systems count in binary units, where each step is 1,024, as this converter does. Measured that way, a 1 TB drive is about 931 GB. Nothing is missing: the same number of bytes is being described in two different units.

About the units

  • Bits: The smallest unit of data: a single 0 or 1.
  • Bytes: 8 bits. Enough to store one character of plain text.
  • Kilobytes: 1,024 bytes in this converter.
  • Megabytes: 1,024 kilobytes. A typical photo is a few megabytes.
  • Gigabytes: 1,024 megabytes. Phone and laptop storage is sold in gigabytes.
  • Terabytes: 1,024 gigabytes. Used for external and network drives.
