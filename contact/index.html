Contact Cubit.tools

Questions, corrections and ideas for new tools are all welcome.

Email: support@cubit.tools

We read every message and aim to reply within two working days.

Report a problem with a tool

If a tool gives a result you think is wrong, or a page does not work properly, please include:

  • the address of the page;
  • what you entered and the result you were given;
  • the result you expected, and why;
  • the device and browser you were using.

Reports of incorrect results are dealt with before anything else.

Suggest a new tool

Tell us what you would like to calculate, convert or count down to. A short description of the inputs and the answer you need is enough. We cannot build every suggestion, but each one is considered when we plan new tools.

Privacy and data requests

The tools on this site run in your browser and we do not hold an account or a profile for you. If you have a question about privacy, cookies or advertising, read our Privacy Policy or email us at the address above.

Advertising and business enquiries

For advertising, partnership or press enquiries, use the same email address and put the subject of your enquiry in the subject line.

What we cannot help with

We cannot give personal financial, tax, legal, medical or electrical advice, and we cannot recover a password created with one of our generators: passwords are made in your browser and are never sent to us or stored.
