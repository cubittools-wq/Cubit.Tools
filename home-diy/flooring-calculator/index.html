How to work out how much flooring you need

Laminate, engineered wood and click vinyl are sold in packs, and each pack states the area it covers. Measure the room at its longest and widest points, multiply the two to get the area, add an allowance for offcuts and divide by the coverage of a pack.

How to use the calculator

  1. Measure the room at its longest and widest points.
  2. Enter the coverage printed on the pack, in square metres.
  3. Set the allowance for waste and enter the price of a pack.

Flooring formula

Floor area = Room length x Room width

Packs = Floor area x (1 + Allowance / 100) / Coverage per pack, rounded up.

Worked example

A living room is 4.5 m long and 3.6 m wide, so the floor area is 16.20 m². Each pack covers 2.22 m². With 10% added for waste, you need 9 packs. At £32 a pack, the flooring costs £288.

How much waste to allow

  • 5% to 10% for boards laid straight in a simple rectangular room.
  • 10% to 15% for rooms with alcoves, chimney breasts or several doorways.
  • 15% or more for herringbone or diagonal patterns.

Other things to budget for

The boards are only part of the cost. You may also need underlay, a damp-proof membrane on a concrete floor, door bars for each doorway, and beading or new skirting boards to cover the expansion gap at the edges. Leave unopened packs in the room for at least 48 hours before fitting, so the boards adjust to the temperature and humidity.

Frequently asked questions

My room is not a rectangle. What do I do?

Divide it into rectangles, work out the area of each and add them up. Enter the total as the length, with a width of 1.

Does this work for carpet?

No. Carpet is sold off a roll of fixed width, usually 4 or 5 metres, so it is priced by the length cut.
