How to calculate the area of a room

For a rectangular room, measure the length and the width in metres and multiply them together. The answer is the floor area in square metres. Measure wall to wall at floor level, and ignore skirting boards.

How to use the calculator

  1. Measure the longest wall and enter it as the length.
  2. Measure the wall at right angles to it and enter that as the width.
  3. Read off the area in square metres and square feet.

A laser measure is quicker and more accurate than a tape in a furnished room.

Area formula

Area (m²) = Length (m) x Width (m)

Area (sq ft) = Area (m²) x 10.7639

Distance around the room = 2 x (Length + Width)

Worked example

A bedroom is 4.2 m long and 3.5 m wide. Its floor area is 14.70 m², which is 158.2 sq ft. The distance around the walls is 15.40 m.

Rooms that are not rectangles

  • L-shaped rooms: split the floor into two rectangles, work out each area and add them together.
  • Alcoves and bay windows: measure them as separate rectangles and add them on.
  • Chimney breasts: measure the area they take up and subtract it.

What the figures are used for

Floor area tells you how much flooring, underlay or underfloor heating to buy, and estate agents quote it when describing a property. The distance around the room is what you need for skirting board, coving and wallpaper. For paint, multiply the distance around the room by the ceiling height to get the wall area.

Frequently asked questions

I measured in feet. Can I still use this?

Convert each measurement to metres first by multiplying feet by 0.3048. A room of 14 feet by 12 feet is 4.27 m by 3.66 m.

How accurate do I need to be?

Measure to the nearest centimetre. A small error on each side becomes a larger error once the two are multiplied.
