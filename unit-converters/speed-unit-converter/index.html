Speed and Velocity Conversion

Converting speed measurements is vital for travel planning, aviation, maritime navigation, and sports analytics. Effortlessly switch between terrestrial speeds (mph, km/h) and nautical or aviation units (knots).

Common Speed Conversion Rates

  • 1 Mile per Hour (mph) = 1.60934 Kilometers per Hour (km/h)
  • 1 Knot = 1.15078 Miles per Hour (mph)
  • 1 Meter per Second (m/s) = 3.6 Kilometers per Hour (km/h)
