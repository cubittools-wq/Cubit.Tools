Metric and Imperial Length Converter

Metric and imperial systems use different units to measure length. The metric system includes units such as millimetres, centimetres, metres and kilometres, while imperial measurements commonly include inches, feet, yards and miles.

A metric to imperial converter can be useful when working with measurements from different countries, comparing product dimensions, planning distances or simply checking a measurement in another unit.

How to Convert Length Measurements

To convert a length, enter the measurement into the calculator and select the unit you are converting from and the unit you want to convert to. The Length Unit Converter calculates the equivalent measurement instantly.

For example, 1 metre is equal to approximately 3.281 feet, while 1 kilometre is approximately 0.621 miles.
