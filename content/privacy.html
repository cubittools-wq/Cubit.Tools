Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

This policy explains what information is and is not collected when you use Cubit.tools, how cookies and advertising work on the site, and the choices you have.

1. The short version

  • Our tools run in your browser. What you type into them is not sent to us.
  • You do not need an account, and we do not ask for your name or email address to use the site.
  • The site is funded by advertising. Advertising partners, including Google, may use cookies. You can control this.

2. Information you enter into our tools

Calculators, converters, countdowns and password generators on Cubit.tools work entirely within your web browser. The numbers, dates, text and options you enter are processed on your own device. They are not transmitted to our servers, and we cannot see or store them. Passwords and passphrases are generated on your device and are never sent anywhere.

3. Information stored on your device

We use your browser's local storage, which is similar to a cookie but is never sent to a server, to remember:

  • whether you chose the light or dark theme;
  • dates you enter into personal countdowns, such as a birthday or holiday, so that the countdown is still there when you return.

You can remove this at any time by clearing the site data for Cubit.tools in your browser settings.

4. Server logs

Like almost every website, the company that hosts Cubit.tools automatically records technical information when a page is requested: your IP address, the page requested, the date and time, and your browser type. These logs are used to keep the site secure and running. We do not use them to identify individual visitors.

5. Advertising and cookies

Cubit.tools uses Google AdSense to show advertisements on some pages. When adverts are shown:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and other sites on the internet.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can opt out of some other vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info or, in Europe, youronlinechoices.com.

To learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

If you visit from the United Kingdom, the European Economic Area or Switzerland, you will be asked for your consent before advertising cookies are set, and you can change your choice later. If you decline, you may still see adverts, but they will not be based on your interests.

6. Analytics

We may use an analytics service to count visits and see which tools are used most, so that we can improve the site. Where such a service uses cookies, it is covered by the same consent choice as advertising cookies. Analytics information is used in aggregate and not to identify you.

7. Controlling cookies in your browser

Every modern browser lets you block or delete cookies and local storage. Blocking cookies does not stop any tool on Cubit.tools from working. Preferences saved on your device, such as the theme, will be forgotten if you clear site data.

8. Contacting us by email

If you email us, we use your email address and the content of your message only to reply and to deal with your request. We do not add you to a mailing list or pass your details to anyone else.

9. Your rights

Under UK and EU data protection law you have the right to ask what personal data is held about you, to have it corrected or deleted, and to object to its use. Because our tools do not collect personal data, there is usually nothing for us to provide, but you can contact us at any time. You also have the right to complain to your data protection authority. In the UK this is the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

10. Children

Cubit.tools is a general-audience site. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Links to other websites

Pages and adverts may link to other websites. We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of those sites, and you should read their own policies.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the site or the law changes. The date at the top of the page shows when it was last revised.

13. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to support@cubit.tools or through our contact page.
