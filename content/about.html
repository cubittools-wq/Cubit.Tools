About Cubit.tools

Free calculators, converters, countdowns and generators that run in your browser, with no sign-up and nothing to install.

What Cubit.tools is

Cubit.tools is an independent website that brings small, useful online tools together in one place. Each tool does one job: working out a profit margin, converting a measurement, counting down to a date or creating a strong password you can remember. Every page also explains the method behind the tool, with the formula and a worked example, so you can check the result for yourself.

The name comes from the cubit, one of the oldest units of measurement: the length of a forearm, from the elbow to the tip of the middle finger. It was a simple tool that anyone could use, which is what we want every page on this site to be.

How the tools work

Every tool runs entirely in your web browser. The numbers, dates and options you enter are not sent to us and are not stored on a server. Password generators use your browser's built-in cryptographic random number generator, and the passwords they create never leave your device.

Because nothing is processed on a server, the tools are fast, and they keep working on a slow connection once the page has loaded.

How we check accuracy

  • Formulas are shown on the page. You can see how each result is worked out.
  • Worked examples are tested. The example in each article uses the same figures as the tool, and we check that the two agree before a page is published.
  • Dates and rates are checked against official sources, such as GOV.UK for bank holidays and VAT rates. Dates set by the moon or by local announcement can differ by a day in some places, and we say so where that applies.

Our tools are a guide for everyday use. They are not professional financial, tax, legal, medical or electrical advice. For a decision that matters, check the result with a qualified professional.

How the site is funded

Cubit.tools is free to use. The site is paid for by advertising, which is shown on some pages and kept clear of the tools themselves. We do not sell personal data, and no tool is hidden behind a payment or a sign-up form. Our Privacy Policy explains how advertising cookies work and how to control them.

Suggest a tool or report a problem

New tools are added regularly, and many of them start as a suggestion from a visitor. If there is a calculator or converter you would find useful, or you have spotted a mistake, please tell us through the contact page. Reports of wrong results are looked at first.
