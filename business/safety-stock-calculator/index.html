What is safety stock?

Safety stock is the extra stock you keep in case sales are higher than expected or a delivery arrives late. It is a buffer against running out.

Safety stock formula

This calculator uses the average and maximum method:

Safety stock = (Maximum daily sales x Maximum lead time) - (Average daily sales x Average lead time)

Reorder point = (Average daily sales x Average lead time) + Safety stock

Worked example

You normally sell 20 units a day and your supplier normally takes 7 days to deliver. On your busiest days you sell 30 units, and the slowest delivery took 10 days. You should hold 160 units of safety stock and place a new order when stock falls to 300 units.

Things to bear in mind

  • The method covers the worst case you have already seen. It cannot predict a bigger spike or a longer delay.
  • More safety stock means more cash tied up and more storage, so review the figures when sales patterns change.
  • Seasonal products need different figures at different times of year.
