What is click-through rate?

Click-through rate, or CTR, is the percentage of people who click something after seeing it. It applies to adverts, organic search results, email links and buttons on a web page.

CTR formula

CTR = Clicks / Impressions x 100

Worked example

An advert is shown 12,000 times and clicked 240 times. Its CTR is 2%.

Why CTR matters

A higher CTR means your message matches what the audience is looking for. On pay-per-click platforms it can also lower what you pay, because adverts that are clicked more often are treated as more relevant. In organic search, a better title and description can win more clicks from the same ranking.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good CTR?

It depends on the channel. Search adverts usually get a much higher CTR than display banners, because people are already looking for something. Compare like with like.

Is a high CTR always good?

Not if the clicks don't convert. An advert that attracts the wrong visitors costs money without producing sales.
