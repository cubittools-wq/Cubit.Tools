What is burn rate?

Burn rate is the amount of cash a business uses up each month. It matters most to start-ups and to any company spending more than it earns, because it shows how long the money will last.

Burn rate formula

Monthly burn rate = (Starting cash - Current cash) / Months

Cash runway = Current cash / Monthly burn rate

This is net burn: what you spend less what you take in.

Worked example

Your bank balance has fallen from £180,000 to £120,000 over 6 months. You are burning £10,000 a month, which leaves a runway of 12.0 months at the current rate.

Gross burn and net burn

Gross burn is everything you spend in a month. Net burn takes off the income you received. A business spending £40,000 a month and taking £30,000 in sales has a gross burn of £40,000 and a net burn of £10,000. Runway depends on net burn.

Extending your runway

  • Cut or delay costs that do not bring in customers.
  • Collect payments sooner and agree longer terms with suppliers.
  • Raise prices or focus on the most profitable products.
  • Start fundraising well before the cash runs low, as it usually takes months.
