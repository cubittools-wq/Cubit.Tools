What is CPA?

CPA, cost per acquisition (or cost per action), is the average amount you pay in advertising for each conversion. A conversion is whatever you count as success: a sale, a sign-up, an enquiry or an app install.

CPA formula

CPA = Campaign cost / Number of conversions

Worked example

You spend £1,200 on a campaign that produces 48 orders. Your CPA is £25.

Is your CPA affordable?

Compare it with the profit from a conversion. If an average order leaves £40 of gross profit, a CPA of £25 still makes money on the first sale, while a CPA of £50 only pays back if customers return and buy again. Working out customer lifetime value shows how much you can afford to pay.

How to lower CPA

  • Pause keywords, audiences and placements that spend without converting.
  • Improve the landing page so more visitors convert.
  • Test new adverts regularly against your best performer.
