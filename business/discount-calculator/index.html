Discount formula

Saving = Original price x Discount / 100

Sale price = Original price - Saving

Worked example

An item priced at £80 with 25% off is reduced by £20, so the sale price is £60.

What a discount does to your margin

A discount comes straight out of your profit, because your costs stay the same. If an item costs you £60 and sells for £80, your profit is £20. Take 25% off and the price drops to £60: the whole profit has gone, even though the discount was only a quarter of the price.

Before running a promotion, work out how many extra sales you need for the lower profit per sale to add up to more profit overall.

Frequently asked questions

How do stacked discounts work?

They are applied one after another, not added together. 20% off followed by a further 10% off is 28% off in total, not 30%.

How do I find the original price from a sale price?

Divide the sale price by (1 - discount / 100). An item that costs £60 after 25% off was £80.
